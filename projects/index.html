COIN: Component-Aware Operator Learning for Shell Design

Feb 2025–May 2026

Developed separate networks for radial and axial displacement prediction, reducing radial relative L2 error by approximately 30% on average across three neural-operator architectures against their shared-network baselines. Combined geometry-conditioned predictions with a genetic algorithm to minimize structural volume under a displacement constraint.

First-author manuscript being revised for resubmission.
