About me

I am Yuxi Zhai, a master’s student in mechanics at Shanghai University, with a research focus on fluid mechanics. I received my B.Sc. in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from Qian Weichang College, Shanghai University, in 2025. I expect to complete my master’s degree in 2028.

My research connects scientific machine learning, neural operators and fluid dynamics. I develop geometry-conditioned surrogate models, investigate how neural operators handle irregular finite-element meshes, and combine learned flow predictions with discrete physical constraints.

My current work includes variable-geometry cylinder flows using FEniCSx and Transolver, and neural-operator methods for structural field prediction. I am the first author of two manuscripts: COIN, being revised for resubmission, and a geometry-conditioned operator study in preparation.
